Version 0.1.0 - First public release on F-Droid!

Features:
- Binary-clock home-screen widget: hours, minutes, day and month as a grid of dots
- AMOLED-black monochrome aesthetic
- Alarm-state glyph mirroring the system alarm indicator
- Optional weather glyph via the keyless Open-Meteo API
- Configurable dot, icon and background colours; optional Material You theming
