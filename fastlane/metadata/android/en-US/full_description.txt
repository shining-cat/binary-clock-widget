BinClockWidget is an AMOLED-black, monochrome binary-clock home-screen widget. It renders the current time and date as a grid of dots — solid on true black, no chrome, no colour — so it disappears into an AMOLED wallpaper and sips battery.

How to read it:

The face is a grid of rows (values) and columns (place values).
* Rows encode, top to bottom: hours, minutes, day of month, month.
* Columns are binary place values: 32, 16, 8, 4, 2, 1.
* A lit dot means that place value is on; add the lit columns in a row to read that row's number.

The cells not needed by the shorter rows are reused to show the device alarm state and a weather-condition glyph.

Features:
* Pure binary time and date display
* Alarm-state glyph that mirrors the system alarm indicator
* Optional weather glyph (current, today, tomorrow)
* Configurable dot, icon and background colours; optional Material You theming
* AMOLED-friendly true black, no unnecessary redraws

Privacy and openness:

BinClockWidget is 100% free and open-source software with no Google Play Services, no Firebase, and no proprietary SDKs. Weather uses the keyless Open-Meteo API; location uses the platform LocationManager (no fused/Play location).
